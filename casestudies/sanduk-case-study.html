01 — Overview

The Brief

The Sanduk is a vibrant fashion brand rooted in cultural heritage — built around the idea of a treasure chest overflowing with bold, wearable art. The project required building a complete brand identity from scratch: a visual language that could carry both tradition and modernity with confidence.

As a marketer, branding The Sanduk was an inspiring journey — translating rich cultural roots into a bold, modern identity that feels both meaningful and irresistibly stylish.

The deliverables spanned logo design, full visual identity, website design, and a social media content palette — all built to scale consistently across every touchpoint.

03 — Logo & Concept

Symbol &
meaning.

The logo represents a vibrant bag icon with colours emerging out of it — a deliberate visual metaphor symbolising the blend of tradition and innovation.

The Sanduk (meaning "chest" or "box") is rooted in cultural heritage — the idea of a treasure chest overflowing with creative, wearable art. The logo carries this story without needing words.

The tagline — "From Mind To Fashion" — communicates the brand's core promise: that every piece begins with an idea and ends as a statement.
