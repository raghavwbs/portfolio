01 — Overview

The Brief

CAMBIO is a consultancy focused on social entrepreneurship and sustainable leadership — operating as a House of Social Change. When I joined, the site had significant SEO issues holding back its organic visibility and digital reach.

I was brought in as Digital Marketing Executive to audit, fix, and grow the site's search performance — from a cluttered 26-page architecture down to a clean, crawlable 8-page structure, with measurable results in organic traffic and worldwide visibility.

03 — Solutions

What I did.

✓
Decluttered Site Architecture
Reduced the site from 26 pages down to 8 focused pages — improving navigation, page load times, and SEO crawl efficiency. Each remaining page was given a clear purpose, strong CTAs, and improved readability.
✓
Meta Descriptions & Title Tags
Wrote and implemented keyword-rich meta titles and descriptions for every page using Yoast SEO, ensuring proper SERP representation and click-through optimisation for all major search engines.
✓
Google Crawlers Optimisation
Fixed domain redirect issues (/2020), optimised robots.txt, and ensured all key pages were fully indexable by Google and Bing. Crawlability confirmed via Google Search Console.
✓
Keyword Research & Mapping
Built a comprehensive keyword research spreadsheet (stored on SharePoint) using a custom ChatGPT prompt — covering 20+ high-impact UK keywords per blog with theme, intent, volume, and CPC data. Mapped keywords to Yoast focus keyphrase fields.
✓
Image Optimisation
Compressed and re-formatted all images across the site, added descriptive alt tags, and implemented lazy loading to improve page performance scores.
✓
Content Alignment & Blog SEO
Restructured blog content around focus keyphrases. Ensured keyphrase density, subheadings, internal links, and content length aligned with Yoast green-light requirements across all published posts.
