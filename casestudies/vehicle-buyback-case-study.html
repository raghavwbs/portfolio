02 — Problems Faced & Solved

I built this campaign.
Here's what I solved along the way.

I planned, launched, and ran this three-campaign funnel end to end — top-of-funnel awareness, remarketing, and page growth. Here's what I ran into along the way, and what I built to fix it.

🎯
Cold reach was too expensive

Problem faced: the awareness campaign was optimised for Quote Accepts, so every cold impression competed for an expensive direct conversion.

Change I brought in: built the campaign around Maximum Reach instead, letting remarketing handle conversion downstream.

Result: reach up 74%, spend down 67%.

🎨
Remarketing creative had gone stale

Problem faced: ad creative shown to warm, returning visitors no longer matched current branding and messaging.

Change I brought in: planned and shipped a full creative refresh for the remarketing audience.

Result: cost per quote accept fell to £8.33, its lowest point all year.

⚖️
Budget wasn't matched to performance

Problem faced: daily spend was split across three campaigns with no clear read on which ones deserved more weight.

Change I brought in: rebuilt the budget allocation from £180/day to £115/day based on what was actually converting.

Result: 36% less spend, with output held or improved.

🎬
Page growth relied on paid production

Problem faced: follower-growth ads leaned on commissioned creative, pushing cost per follower up.

Change I brought in: rebuilt the campaign around existing team-reel footage instead.

Result: cost per follower down 75%, from £1.13 to £0.28.

06 — Key Insights

What the data
tells us.

📈
Reach-Optimising TOF Was the Big Unlock

Switching the TOF objective from Quote Accepts to Reach lifted reach 74% (438K vs. 252K) while cutting spend 67% (£2,435 vs. £7,442) over the same four-month window — proof that TOF shouldn't be judged on conversion cost.

🔁
Remarketing Carries the Funnel

At £11.98 per quote accept, remarketing consistently outperforms cold TOF spend on direct conversions — because it draws on users who already showed intent via the Pixel.

🎨
April's Creative Refresh Paid Off

Updating remarketing creative to current branding in April 2026 dropped Cost/Quote Accept to its yearly low of £8.33, before rising media costs pushed it back up through Q2.

📉
Page Growth Got 4x More Efficient

Cost per follow/profile visit fell steadily from £1.13 to £0.28 across 12 months — even as the daily budget was cut in half — by rotating in existing team-reel content instead of paid production.

⚡
Total Spend Cut 36%, Output Held

Rebalancing the daily budget from £180 to £115 across TOF, remarketing and page growth preserved — and in TOF's case, improved — output, freeing budget for further testing.

🎯
Full-Funnel Pixel Tracking Enabled All of This

Page View, Initiate Checkout, and Lead events were verified live across every landing page — the remarketing audience simply doesn't exist without that instrumentation being correct first.
