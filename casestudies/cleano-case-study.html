01 — Overview

The Brief

(Cleano) was conceptualised as a modern cleaning brand focused on delivering efficient and eco-friendly cleaning solutions. The objective was to create a brand that communicates hygiene, reliability, and sustainability while maintaining strong visual consistency across packaging and promotional materials.

The brand needed to feel premium but approachable — a home care brand that elevates everyday cleaning with naturally derived formulas housed in sustainable, minimalist packaging. Clean aesthetics that blended high-performance cleaning with modern design sensibility.

I designed the complete branding system — logo, visual identity, colour palette, typography, packaging design, marketing assets, and social media visual strategy.

02 — Logo & Concept

The mark.

Logo Rationale

The (Cleano) logotype uses parentheses as a distinctive brand device — wrapping the name in a clean, structural embrace that communicates containment, precision, and order.

The circular bracket form echoes the brand's core values: completeness, protection, and cycle-driven sustainability.
