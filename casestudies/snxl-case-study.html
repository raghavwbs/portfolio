01 — Overview

The Brief

SkateNationXL is a Web3 skateboarding game developed by MBS Studios — a physics-based multiplayer experience with deep customisation and competitive gameplay. The challenge: launch the game to a global audience with a trailer that could generate hype, drive Steam wishlists, and build a community before release.

I led the end-to-end production process — from conceptualising the narrative and writing the script, to coordinating with external studios, managing timelines, and delivering the final trailer across platforms.

The result: a cinematic reveal that picked up 1.2M+ views across channels, coverage from IGN and major gaming outlets, 8k+ Steam wishlists, and a Discord community of 10k+ verified members.

04 — Creative Direction

Trailer script
excerpt.

Scene 01 — Opening

The trailer opens with a breathtaking, almost dizzying high-angle shot. We're positioned impossibly high, looking straight down into a sprawling, hyper-futuristic metropolis. Towering gleaming skyscrapers pierce atmospheric clouds, bathed in the warm golden glow of a perpetual dawn or dusk.

Scene 02 — Introduction

Transition to a first-person perspective — the viewer placed directly onto the edge. We see the player's feet, clad in sleek high-tech footwear, firmly planted on a cutting-edge hover-skateboard. Below, the dizzying cityscape stretches endlessly, reinforcing the precarious height.

Scene 03 — Action

Without hesitation, the skater launches off the precipice. The camera follows as they plummet, the city rushing up at incredible speed. The board's unique hover capabilities become apparent — subtle energy trails emanating from its underside, showcasing our innovative movement system.

Scene 04 — Gameplay Montage

Rapid-fire footage of spectacular gameplay — grinding impossibly long rails, weaving through dense aerial traffic, performing tricks between closely packed buildings. A shot of multiple players racing side-by-side, emphasising the multiplayer and competitive elements.
